## 4. Analysis of UNKNOWN Samples

- **Total Classified as UNKNOWN:** `2790` images (71.0% of inspected images).
- **Core Reasons for UNKNOWN Assignment:**
  1. **Strict Uncertainty Policy:** Images with confidence `< 0.35` were systematically assigned to `UNKNOWN` rather than forced into speculative categories.
  2. **Inter-Category Ambiguity:** Screenshots where top category scores were within a tight margin (`< 0.05`), such as mixed e-commerce payment checkout screens or social feed payment transfers, were classified as `UNKNOWN`.
  3. **Cropped / Minimal Content:** Images featuring zoomed-in text snippets without viewport headers, footers, or distinct UI navigation frames.

---

## 5. Limitations of Scene Classification

1. **Absence of Ground Truth:** STFD was designed and released exclusively for text forgery localization. Scene labels were not released with the dataset.
2. **Language Pack Constraints:** Host OCR operates via Windows Media OCR in English/Latin mode, extracting numbers, Latin terms, brand names, and symbols, while Chinese glyphs are partially represented as phonetic/structural tokens.
3. **Hybrid Application Screens:** Real-world smartphones frequently feature cross-domain screens (e.g., in-app payment inside social chat, shopping cart checkout inside a web browser), which intentionally yield conservative confidence scores.
